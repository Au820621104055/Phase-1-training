About the Movie - Coolie

Coolie is a popular Indian Tamil-language action drama film starring Rajinikanth in the lead role. The movie is known for its high-energy action sequences, powerful dialogues, and Rajinikanth’s charismatic performance.

The story revolves around a hardworking coolie (porter) who rises against injustice, stands for the common people, and fights against corruption and oppression. It is remembered for its blend of action, drama, comedy, and emotion, making it a family entertainer.

Highlights:

  • Genre: Action / Drama
  • Lead Actor: Rajinikanth
  • Memorable songs and powerful background score
  • Famous for Rajini’s mass screen presence

The film has left a lasting impact on Tamil cinema and is loved by fans for its entertainment value and the superstar’s unique style.
